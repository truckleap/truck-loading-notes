---
title: "Liftgate and Dock Questions for 26-Foot Box Trucks"
description: "What to confirm about the delivery point before booking a straight-truck stop."
---

A 26-foot box truck can reach places a 53-foot trailer cannot, which is why so much of its freight goes to stores, job sites and homes. Those same places are where deliveries go wrong. Most problems come down to a few questions nobody asked when the load was booked.

## Is there a dock, and is it the right height?

A standard dock sits about 48 to 52 inches off the ground. Many box trucks have a deck height closer to 44 to 48 inches, and a dock leveler usually covers the gap. Ask whether the receiver has a working leveler or dock plate. If the answer is "we have a dock, but it's for trailers," plan on the liftgate.

## What does the liftgate need to lift?

Check the rated capacity on the gate. Common straight-truck gates are rated 2,500 to 3,000 lb, measured with the load centered on the platform. A 1,800 lb pallet hanging off the edge is a different story. Ask for the heaviest single piece and the pallet size, not just the total weight.

## Who unloads?

"Liftgate delivery" usually means the driver lowers freight to the ground at the tailgate. It does not mean inside delivery, stairs, or setting up equipment. If the receiver expects any of those, it is a separate charge, and it should be on the rate confirmation before you accept.

## Can the truck get in?

Residential streets, strip-mall alleys and downtown loading zones all have their own limits:

- Low branches and wires near homes (a box is around 12 to 13 feet tall)
- Weight-limit signs on side streets
- Delivery-hour windows set by the property manager
- No room to back in, so the gate opens into traffic

A street-view check takes two minutes and saves a failed delivery.

## Accessorials to ask about up front

| Service | Typical question |
|---|---|
| Liftgate | Is it required at pickup, delivery, or both? |
| Inside delivery | How far past the door, and how many floors? |
| Residential | Is the stop a home or home-based business? |
| Appointment | Is there a fixed time or a window? |
| Detention | When does the clock start and what is the hourly rate? |
